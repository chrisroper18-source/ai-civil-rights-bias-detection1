How it works

From evidence to review

The system checks for patterns that may indicate bias, then sends the case to humans for a final decision.

1. Input

Bodycam footage, CCTV, reports, filings, transcripts, and notes enter the system.

2. Detection

The AI scans for patterns that may indicate over-policing, unequal treatment, or inconsistent reporting.

3. Human Review

A six-member panel with different backgrounds reviews the alert and decides the next step.

4. Action

Cases may move to complaint filing, legal review, policy correction, or internal audit.

Atlanta example

Real-world scenario

What the system detects

A Black driver is repeatedly stopped near downtown Atlanta or near Hartsfield-Jackson. The system compares footage, stop reports, and location patterns against prior examples and flags possible racial profiling.

Why Atlanta works

Atlanta makes the example concrete because real civil-rights disputes have involved airport stops and discriminatory treatment concerns in the city.

Human panel

Six members, different backgrounds

A diverse review panel helps reduce blind spots and improves the quality of discussion.

Why diversity matters

Research shows diverse juries deliberate longer, discuss more facts, and make fewer factual errors than all-white juries.

What the panel does

It reviews the AI alert, considers context, and decides whether the case should be escalated or referred for further review.
